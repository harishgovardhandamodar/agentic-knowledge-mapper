AI Security Engineering & Evaluation Agent

Threat modelling and residual-risk scoring for AI-assisted data workflows.

Three separate assessments with their own agents, weights and scores — run whichever you need, or all of them. Security landscape

Model security as a knowledge base for this investigation: inventory, the own / inherited / cascade partition, exploits, engineering insights, coverage gaps and selection aids. Reads stored assessment rows — nothing here re-scores or executes. Risk from literature is evidence, not a measured production residual.
A decision writes an edge with actor and rationale. It never changes a score.

Portfolio risk

One register across product, model, privacy and supply-chain rows. Every row names the assessment and catalog it came from, and reports its own control coverage split into evidenced and declared only. Severity is comparable within a layer; the layers are not collapsed into one number. Nothing here marks a risk secured.

Leadership Dashboard

Two layers: the assurance position (verified residual, decision queue, system health) and the portfolio register behind it.
The assurance board answers "what must a person decide, and what do we believe it?". The register answers "is the portfolio covered and owned?".

Portfolio register operations

The management plane on top of the analyst Landscape: availability (do we know enough?), distribution (where does risk sit?), robustness (how well are risks owned, mitigated, validated, kept current?). Read-only aggregates — every percent carries its sample size, unknowns inflate attention rather than robustness, and no widget implies measured production risk.
